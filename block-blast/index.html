HOW TO PLAY

Rules, Combos & Tips

  • 🧩 Drag & Drop pieces from the tray onto the 8x8 grid. Or tap a piece, then tap where you want it placed.
  • 💥 Blast Lines: Fill complete rows or columns to clear them and score big points!
  • 🔥 Combo Streaks: Clear lines on consecutive turns to build huge multipliers (x2, x3, x4+).
  • 🔨 Power-ups: Use the Hammer to smash blocks, Swap to get new pieces, or Rotate All (unlimited) to turn all 3 tray pieces.
  • ⚠️ Game Over: If none of the remaining pieces can fit anywhere on the board, game is over!
