# THE BARROW OF THE LANTERN KING
### Fighting Fantasy: The Lost Volumes, Book 1

---

## BACKGROUND

For three hundred years the ruined watchtower on Blackcrag has stood empty above the village of Hollowmere. Then, this autumn, the lights began: a sickly green lantern-glow moving behind the broken arrow-slits after midnight. Since then, four villagers have walked up the hill in their sleep, and none have come back.

The village elder presses ten Gold Pieces into your hand and tells you the old story. The tower was built over the barrow of **Morgrath**, a drowned warlord who bound his soul into a lantern so that he would never have to die. The story says that iron and steel pass through him like smoke. Only *silver* can bite.

You have your sword, your leather armour, a backpack with four days' Provisions, and your wits. Night is falling. You climb Blackcrag alone.

> **RULES:** Your SKILL, STAMINA and LUCK are recorded on your Adventure Sheet. If your STAMINA ever reaches 0, your adventure is over. You may eat a Provision at any time when not in combat to restore 4 STAMINA.

---

## 1

The wind screams through the ruined watchtower as you shoulder open its rotten door. Inside, the floor has fallen in, leaving only a ring of slick stone around a square black trapdoor. It is open, and ancient iron rungs lead down into darkness. A faint green light flickers somewhere below and then goes out.

You light your lantern and climb down. Forty rungs later your boots touch flagstones. You are standing in a low vaulted hall that smells of grave-dirt and cold ash. Carved skulls grin at you from every pillar.

There are two ways out. To the west, a stone archway leads into darkness, and the air there smells of old iron and rust. To the east, a stair spirals steeply downward, its steps cracked and crumbling. From far below comes the low, endless rush of running water.

If you take the west archway, turn to **29**.
If you descend the east stair, turn to **90**.

---

## 2

The bronze doors groan open onto a vast throne room, flooded ankle-deep in black water. Pillars rise like drowned trees into the darkness. At the far end, on a throne of barnacled stone, sits a towering figure in rusted plate armour. His face is a skull wrapped in grey, waterlogged flesh. In one gauntleted fist he holds a lantern, and inside it a green flame writhes like something alive.

Behind the throne, chained to the wall, the four missing villagers sway in an enchanted sleep.

The figure rises. Water pours from his armour. His voice is the sound of a river closing over your head.

*"Another hero,"* hisses **Morgrath the Lantern King**. *"Your steel will pass through me like a blade through smoke. Come, then. Add your light to mine."*

If you have the **Silver Sickle**, turn to **96**.
If you do not, turn to **35**.

---

## 3

The narrow stair opens into a small chapel. Seawater has seeped through its walls over the centuries, and pale crusts of salt cover the pews like frost. A cracked altar stands at the far end, and above it is a faded mural of a robed priestess holding a curved silver blade up against a green flame.

A shallow offering bowl rests on the altar, green with age. Something about this place feels watchful, but not hostile.

If you wish, you may place 1 Gold Piece in the bowl (you may do this only once). If you do, a faint warmth passes through you. Add 1 LUCK point (this may not take you above your *Initial* LUCK).

Behind the altar, a spiral stair winds downward into the dark.

If you descend the spiral stair, turn to **93**.
If you return to the corridor, turn to **47**.

---

## 14

The tunnel opens onto the bank of a wide underground river. The water is black, fast and freezing, and its roar fills the cavern. Your lantern light barely reaches the far bank, where you can just make out stone steps rising towards a pair of tall bronze doors.

Moored nearby is a flat-bottomed boat. A hooded figure sits motionless in it, holding a long pole. Further upstream, the water churns white over a shallow ford of stepping-stones. Something pale and bloated drifts just below the surface there, and then is gone.

To the west, a dry tunnel leads away from the bank.

If you hail the figure in the boat, turn to **55**.
If you wade across the ford, turn to **53**.
If you follow the dry tunnel west, turn to **47**.

---

## 22

The mushroom tastes of copper and moonlight. Warmth floods your limbs. Add 3 STAMINA points (not above your *Initial* STAMINA).

Then the grotto melts away.

You are standing in a rotted armoury you have never seen. Cold air whistles through a gap in the mortar of one wall. Behind the stone, wrapped in oilcloth, something gleams with the pure white shine of *silver*. A woman's voice whispers: *"Only the moon's metal cuts the drowned king."*

The vision fades. You are on your knees in the glowing grotto, gasping, with the taste of copper still on your tongue. You have a feeling that the armoury in the vision lies somewhere to the west of this place.

Shaking, you rise and push on towards the roar of water. Turn to **14**.

---

## 29

Rusted weapon racks line the walls of this long guardroom. In the centre stands a stone table, and slumped across it is a skeleton in a corroded half-helm, as if it fell asleep at its post centuries ago.

As your lantern light falls on it, the skull jerks upright. Green pinpricks of light kindle in its empty eye sockets. With a dry clatter of bones it rises, drags a notched sword from the table, and lurches towards you.

`SKELETON GUARD — SKILL 7  STAMINA 6`

You may *Escape* during the fight by running for the far door. If you do, lose 2 STAMINA as the blade bites your back, and turn to **47**.

If you destroy the Skeleton Guard, turn to **54**.

---

## 35

You raise your sword and charge. Morgrath does not even lift a hand to defend himself.

Your blade passes clean through his breastplate, as if you have struck at fog. Cold flows up the steel and into your arm. Your fingers go numb, and your sword drops into the black water with a dull splash.

The Lantern King lifts his lantern towards your face. The green flame leans out to meet you, hungry. You feel your warmth, your memories and your name being drawn out of you like a thread pulled from cloth.

The last thing you see is your own face, small and screaming, behind the lantern glass.

**Your adventure ends here.**

---

## 47

This long corridor is lined with carved stone faces: kings, priests and warriors, their features worn smooth by damp. As you pass, you have the uneasy feeling that their blank eyes follow you.

The corridor branches. A narrow stair climbs to the north, and you smell salt and old incense. To the east, a damp tunnel slopes downward towards the sound of rushing water. Back to the west is an iron-bound door leading into an old armoury.

If you climb the north stair, turn to **3**.
If you follow the tunnel east, turn to **14**.
If you go west into the armoury, turn to **54**.

---

## 51

You haul yourself up onto the far bank, dripping and shivering. Broad stone steps rise from the water's edge towards the great bronze doors. Lanterns are carved into their surface, and each one has a screaming face where its flame should be.

At the foot of the steps, a body lies tangled in the reeds. It is an adventurer in a rotted leather jerkin, face-down, and a backpack is still strapped to its shoulders.

If you search the body, turn to **85**.
If you climb the steps to the bronze doors, turn to **93**.

---

## 53

You step onto the first slick stone. Freezing water tears at your ankles. Halfway across, the river in front of you erupts.

A bloated grey shape surges up from the depths. It has the body of a drowned man, swollen and white, with a lamprey's mouth full of circling teeth where its face should be. Webbed claws slash at your legs.

`RIVER GHOUL — SKILL 8  STAMINA 7`

**Special Rule:** You are fighting on treacherous footing. If the River Ghoul wins two Attack Rounds in a row, it drags you under the surface, and you lose 1 *extra* STAMINA point. You may not *Escape* from this fight.

If you defeat the River Ghoul, turn to **51**.

---

## 54

The iron-bound door opens into a long, low armoury. Racks of spears and halberds line the walls, but the hafts have rotted to black pulp and the blades have rusted to flaking orange lace. A shield bearing a lantern emblem hangs crookedly on its peg. Nothing here is fit to fight with.

Yet as you turn to leave, your lantern flame gutters and leans sideways. A thin, cold draught is whistling through a gap in the mortar of the far wall, low down near the floor. Where there is a draught, there must be a space behind the stone.

If you examine the wall more closely, turn to **71**.
If you leave by the iron-bound door, turn to **47**.

---

## 55

You raise a hand and call out. Slowly the hooded figure turns. Beneath the hood there is no face, only a smooth oval of grey bone with a single slit for a mouth. A thin, dry voice rattles out of it.

*"Three coins to cross. Three coins, or the river."*

It holds out a skeletal palm and waits.

If you pay 3 Gold Pieces, deduct them from your Adventure Sheet and turn to **51**.
If you refuse (or cannot pay), you step back onto the bank. Turn to **14**.

---

## 59

The stair ends in a vast cavern, and you catch your breath. The ceiling glitters with thousands of glowing blue mushrooms, casting a soft, eerie twilight over pools of still water. Your footsteps echo strangely here, as if someone is walking just behind you.

Near your feet, a cluster of larger mushrooms pulses gently with light, like a sleeping heartbeat. They smell sweet, almost like honey.

Beyond the grotto, you hear the roar of a great river.

If you eat one of the glowing mushrooms, turn to **22**.
If you push on towards the sound of water, turn to **14**.

---

## 63

The step gives way with a crack like a bone snapping. You plunge into darkness, hit a slope of loose rubble, and tumble head over heels before landing hard at the bottom of a deep pit. Lose 4 STAMINA.

Groaning, you relight your lantern. You are lying on a carpet of bones: rats, dogs, and things you would rather not name. Wedged in the ribcage of one ancient skeleton is a small carved charm of yellowed bone, shaped like a closed eye. When you touch it, the lantern flame steadies and burns a little whiter.

You may take the **Bone Charm** if you wish. Add it to your inventory.

Thick roots trail down the pit wall from above. With an effort, you haul yourself up them and stagger into the cavern beyond. Turn to **59**.

---

## 71

You kneel and work your dagger into the crumbling mortar. A block of stone shifts, then slides free with a grinding scrape, and an icy breath of air rushes out at you.

Behind the stone is a small, dry alcove. Inside lies a bundle wrapped in oilcloth and tied with a cord of faded blue silk. You unwrap it with trembling fingers.

It is a sickle, its curved blade made of pure, untarnished **silver** that gleams like captured moonlight. Runes of warding run along its edge. Someone hid this here long ago for the one who would come to face the Lantern King.

Add the **Silver Sickle** to your inventory. You may use it in place of your sword.

You slip back through the armoury and out of the iron-bound door. Turn to **47**.

---

## 85

You roll the body over and immediately wish you hadn't. The face has been nibbled away by river creatures. But the backpack is intact. Inside you find **3 Gold Pieces**, **1 Provision** wrapped in waxed cloth (still edible, remarkably), and a small leather journal swollen with water.

Most of the pages have dissolved into pulp, but the last legible entry reads:

*"…my blade passed through him like smoke. The priestess's sickle. I should have found the priestess's sickle. Cold air, behind the armoury wall…"*

Add the gold, the Provision and the **Waterlogged Journal** to your Adventure Sheet. Then climb the steps to the bronze doors. Turn to **93**.

---

## 90

You start down the spiral stair. The steps are worn into hollows by centuries of feet, and some have broken away completely, leaving black gaps into nothing. Water drips from above. Moss makes every surface treacherous.

Halfway down, you set your weight on a step, and feel it *shift*.

**Test your Luck.**

If you are Lucky, you leap to the next step just as the old one crumbles away beneath your heel. You make it safely to the bottom. Turn to **59**.
If you are Unlucky, turn to **63**.

---

## 93

You stand in a tall, echoing antechamber. Dozens of iron lanterns hang from chains overhead, and every one is dark except a single lamp above the great bronze doors, which burns with a cold green flame. As you watch, the flame twists into the shape of a screaming face and then settles again.

From beyond the doors comes the slow drip of water, and something else: the faint sound of people weeping in their sleep.

You sense that once you pass through these doors, there will be no turning back. If there is anything left to find in this barrow, now is the time.

If you push open the bronze doors, turn to **2**.
If you turn back up the spiral stair, turn to **3**.

---

## 95

The Lantern King falls to his knees in the black water. Light pours out of the cracks in his armour, then darkens and is gone. The empty armour crashes down and sinks. Somewhere far above, you hear a sound like a great sigh.

The chains holding the villagers crumble into rust. One by one they wake, blinking and confused, as if from a long nightmare. Together you climb the iron rungs and emerge onto Blackcrag just as the first grey light of dawn spills over the hills.

Below, the bells of Hollowmere begin to ring.

That night, by the fire in the village inn, the elder raises a tankard in your honour. As the cheering dies down, he leans close and lowers his voice:

*"Morgrath was not the only one who made such a lantern. There were seven of them…"*

**You have completed THE BARROW OF THE LANTERN KING.**

---

## 96

You draw the Silver Sickle. As its moonlit edge catches the green glow, Morgrath *recoils*. For the first time in three hundred years, the Lantern King is afraid.

*"Where did you get that?"* he snarls. Then he hurls himself at you in a storm of black water and rusted steel.

`MORGRATH THE LANTERN KING — SKILL 11  STAMINA 14`

- **Bone Charm:** If you carry the Bone Charm, it blazes white as the fight begins. Morgrath's eye-lights dim, and you may reduce his SKILL by 2 for this entire battle.
- **Lantern Flare:** At the start of every *third* Attack Round, the lantern erupts with green fire. Test your Luck. If you are Unlucky, lose 2 STAMINA.
- **Strike the Lantern:** Instead of fighting Morgrath himself, you may attack the lantern he carries (`THE SOUL LANTERN — SKILL 9  STAMINA 4`). While you do so, Morgrath still attacks you as normal, but your successful hits damage only the lantern. If you shatter it, Morgrath's STAMINA is immediately *halved* (rounded down), and the Lantern Flare no longer happens.

If you defeat the Lantern King, turn to **95**.
