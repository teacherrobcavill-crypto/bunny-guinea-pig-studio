### 👤 Human

- **Size / Speed:** Medium | 30 ft | Darkvision: None

- **Overview:** Versatile and ambitious, humans are the most adaptable of all races.

- **Core Ancestral Traits:** Extra Language, Bonus Skill, Resourceful

- **Visual Aesthetic & Silhouette:** Varied skin tones from sun-baked olive to weathered northern pale; practical layered leather, chain hauberks, weathered travel cloaks with bronze buckles; determined, versatile posture.

- **Subraces & Visual Variants:**

- **Level & Stats:** Level 1 | STR 17, DEX 14, CON 16, INT 11, WIS 13, CHA 12

- **Quirk:** Methodically sharpens his longsword with forty-two even strokes of a whetstone every evening, reciting the names of the fallen Watchmen of Neverwinter under his breath.

- **Secret:** During the Siege of the High Road, he hesitated for three heartbeats when a masked lieutenant of the Black Spider offered him safe passage for his surrounded squad—a shame that burns hotter than any wound.

- **Personal Quest:** Locate Gundren Rockseeker, secure the Lost Forge of Spells in Wave Echo Cave, and prove that an honorable shield can still hold the frontier together.

- **Visual Backstory:**
For twelve winters, Sir Kaelen Dawnshield wore the silver starburst of the Neverwinter Watch, rising from a mud-splattered pikeman to Captain of the Eastgate Vanguard. He learned the art of war not in gilded academies, but in the freezing rain of the High Road—holding shield-walls against Cragmaw goblin ambushes, Blackhaven cutthroats, and undead horrors creeping down from the haunted ridges of Ravencrest. When city politicians slashed frontier patrols to line their own coffers, Kaelen laid his captain’s baton upon the Lord Protector’s oak table and walked out with only his notched longsword and dented kite shield.

His old friend and comrade-in-arms, the dwarven prospector Gundren Rockseeker, sought him out in a smoky dockside tavern with news that could reshape the North: the lost entrance to Wave Echo Cave and the legendary Forge of Spells had finally been found. Hiring Kaelen to escort a wagon of mining supplies down the treacherous Triboar Trail to Phandalin, Gundren rode ahead with a warrior escort—and vanished into the goblin-infested thickets.

Now standing over the arrow-riddled horses of Gundren’s ambush site, Kaelen knows that the shadowy web stretching from Wave Echo Cave to Blackhaven’s underbelly and the volcanic peaks of Dragonspine is tightening around the Sword Coast. He has sworn on his knightly spurs to rescue his friend, break the Black Spider’s grip on Phandalin, and rally a band of heroes capable of facing whatever ancient darkness stirs beneath the realm.

- **Quirk:** Communicates tactical hand-signals using mimicry of twilight nightjars and collects a pinch of soil from every ancient ruin in small glass vials around her belt.

- **Secret:** During a solitary scouting trek three years ago, she stepped inside the outer threshold of the Ziggurat of Kha’zar and lost three days of memory—waking up with the Longbow of Warning clutched in her hands and emerald runes faded into her wrist.

- **Visual Backstory:**

Clad in a whisper-light Mithral Chain Shirt and veiled by Pass Without Trace, she moves through subterranean tunnels and strangler-fig jungles like a breath of cool night air. Her Longbow of Warning hums with ancient elven sentinel magic, alerting her to ambushes before an enemy’s hand even reaches for a hilt.

For five seasons, Sylva has tracked a creeping ecological corruption spreading across the realm: poisoned aquifers beneath Wave Echo Cave, necrotic brambles strangling the woods around Ravencrest Manor, and smuggled serpent-idols flowing through Blackhaven’s docks. Every trail converged southward upon the steaming, mist-shrouded expanse of the Jungle of Kha’zar, where the stepped stone pyramid of the Ziggurat of the Sleeping God rises above the canopy.

As the only horizon scout to survive the jungle’s outer death-gardens and guardian beasts, Sylva has assembled a veteran company of delvers to breach the Ziggurat’s inner sanctum. Before the blood-moon aligns over the temple’s altar and the Sleeping God opens its lidless eyes, her arrows will sever every cultist and aberration standing in the dark.
